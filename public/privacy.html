Privacy

OpportunityRadar is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information. Profile fields used for matching should be processed only for the feature being requested.

Opportunity data.
Opportunity records come from public sources and are stored to power search and verification.
Personalization.
Age, country and skills entered into the matcher are intended to be used for matching. We will avoid collecting more information than the feature requires.
Advertising.
When advertising is introduced, this page and the site's consent experience will be updated to describe the relevant providers and choices. Google requires a certified CMP integrated with the IAB Europe TCF when personalized AdSense ads are served to users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland.
