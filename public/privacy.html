Privacy

OpportunityRadar is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information. Profile fields used for matching are intended to be processed only for the feature being requested.

Opportunity data.
Opportunity records come from public sources and are stored to power search and verification.
Personalization.
Age, country and skills entered into the matcher are intended to be used for matching. We avoid collecting more information than the feature requires.
Operational analytics.
When enabled, OpportunityRadar may record aggregate product events such as match-button use and page paths using Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine. The analytics system is intended for service measurement rather than advertising profiles.
Advertising.
Advertising is not active merely because the site is technically prepared for AdSense. When advertising is introduced, this page and the consent experience will be updated to describe the relevant providers and choices. Google requires a certified CMP integrated with the IAB Europe TCF when personalized AdSense ads are served to users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland.
Third parties.
When a user opens an official opportunity source or other third-party link, that external site controls its own processing and privacy practices.
