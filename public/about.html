How OpportunityRadar works

OpportunityRadar helps people find grants, competitions, hackathons and related opportunities that fit their profile.

Source first.
We prefer official organizer pages and public official APIs. The source is the authority.
Verification checkpoint.
A candidate is checked for source provenance, current status, deadline validity, freshness and eligibility information before it can be recommended.
No invented facts.
If a prize, deadline, eligibility rule or location is not established by the source, we do not present a guess as fact.
AI is an assistant.
Automation can classify, tag, summarize and compare records, but it cannot override source facts or bypass the verification gate.
