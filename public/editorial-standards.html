Editorial & Verification Standards

OpportunityRadar treats official organizer or first-party source pages as the authority. Aggregators may be used for discovery research, but they are not sufficient evidence for a recommendation.

Before an opportunity is shown

The server checks source provenance, verification timestamp, open status, deadline validity, and eligibility evidence. Records that cannot meet the critical checks are rejected or held for review.

AI policy

AI may classify, tag, summarize and compare records. It may not invent deadlines, prizes, eligibility rules, locations or outcomes, and it cannot override a failed verification checkpoint.

Corrections

When a source changes, the automated updater should re-check the record. Users can report factual problems through the contact page.
