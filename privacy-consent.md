# Privacy, Consent, and Ad Readiness

OpportunityRadar is designed for global users. Before enabling advertising, the live site must include an appropriate privacy policy, cookie/consent information, terms, and contact method.

## Google publisher requirements

For personalized Google ads served to users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Google currently requires a Google-certified consent-management platform integrated with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework. Use Google's own Privacy & Messaging/CMP where appropriate.

## Data minimization

- Do not collect personal data unless it is needed for a user-selected feature.
- Profile matching should work without requiring an account.
- Do not store a user's profile by default.
- If saved opportunities or alerts are added later, collect only the minimum data needed and provide deletion controls.

## Transparency

- Disclose Google AdSense/Google advertising technologies when enabled.
- Disclose affiliate links.
- Clearly label sponsored opportunities.
- Explain that opportunity data is source-backed but can change, and users should verify the official source before applying.
