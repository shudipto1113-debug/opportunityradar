# OpportunityRadar Monetization Plan

## Primary: Google AdSense

AdSense is planned as the primary passive monetization layer after the site has substantial original, useful, source-backed content and passes Google's review.

Implementation principles:
- Never place ads where they can be mistaken for navigation, filters, result cards, or official-source links.
- Use responsive ads and consider Auto ads after approval.
- Keep the opportunity result and official application link easy to find.
- Limit ad density so ads do not overwhelm useful content.
- Add a certified consent-management flow for EEA/UK/Switzerland traffic as required by Google's publisher policies.
- Add Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookies/Consent information, and Contact pages.
- Never encourage ad clicks or ask users to click ads.

Important: publisher ID/ad tags are added only after the owner has an approved AdSense account. Until then, use non-serving ad placeholders in development.

## Secondary revenue streams

1. Legitimate affiliate partnerships
   - Relevant: tools, software, courses, travel/relocation resources, application services, productivity products.
   - Show affiliate disclosure near qualifying links.
   - Never rank an opportunity higher because it pays a commission.

2. Sponsored opportunities
   - Clearly label sponsored listings.
   - Sponsorship cannot override the verification gate.
   - Sponsored records must remain subject to the same factual verification rules.

3. Featured but non-paid ranking boosts
   - Do not sell ranking positions. Trust is more valuable than a short-term fee.

4. Optional paid alerts later
   - Free users keep access to the core opportunity finder.
   - Paid tier could add more frequent alerts, advanced tracking, or personal dashboards only after the free product has traction.

5. Donations/support
   - Optional, never required for access.

## Revenue guardrails

- No deceptive ads.
- No paid manipulation of eligibility or match scores.
- No hidden affiliate relationships.
- No fabricated opportunities to increase page count or ad impressions.
- No automatically generated low-value pages solely for SEO traffic.
